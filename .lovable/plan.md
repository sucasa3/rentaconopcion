# Homeowner Dashboard — Reference-Fidelity Correction

## Goal
Bring the real 390px dashboard materially closer to the approved reference: richer, denser, more polished, and more visually alive—without changing canonical data, permissions, calculations, or the approved dashboard hierarchy.

## What will change

### 1. Hero and identity
- Increase the visual prominence of the existing property photo while preserving its crop, address, overlay, and current source.
- Refine the address typography and image overlay to match the reference’s stronger home-identity moment.
- Keep the intelligence strip light and frosted, but improve its depth, spacing, icons, dividers, metric typography, and score-ring presentation.
- Keep compact mobile amounts and neutral unavailable states; never invent movement.

### 2. Address completion state
- Restyle the existing incomplete-address prompt as a compact, integrated continuation of the hero instead of a large competing card.
- Preserve every required address field, validation rule, and action.
- Ensure the completed-home state immediately reveals the richer dashboard composition without fabricated preview data.

### 3. Home Health
- Give the section a stronger editorial heading and clearer separation from the hero.
- Retain one unified Home Health area, with the score as the anchor and systems displayed as compact, softly tinted tiles.
- Strengthen blue icon treatments and semantic green, amber, or red conditions only when canonical evidence supports them.
- Keep missing evidence neutral and never relabel Water Heater as Plumbing.

### 4. Home Care, Home Team, and Ask SuCasa
- Make Home Care’s warm surface, segmented control, task row, and status treatment more closely match the reference while remaining compact.
- Refine Home Team into crisp side-by-side professional tiles with initials or existing photos only; preserve pending, confirmed, and missing states.
- Increase Ask SuCasa’s visual presence with a polished pale-blue panel, orange brand mark, and compact navy action.

### 5. Navigation and page rhythm
- Refine the five-item mobile bar—Home, Value, Care, Team, More—to match the reference’s icon scale, active orange state, spacing, and fixed app-like treatment.
- Tighten vertical rhythm so meaningful content appears within the first two screens at 390px.
- Keep Value details on the dedicated Value page rather than restoring the duplicate dashboard section.

## Explicit exclusions
- No sample values, people, weather, greeting, notifications, movement percentages, ROI, or health claims from the mockup.
- No duplicate Value & Equity dashboard card.
- No changes to Home Score, value/equity math, maintenance logic, relationships, consent, permissions, routes, or data models.

## Verification
- Compare the finished implementation directly against the reference at 390px before any further structural changes.
- Test 320px, 375px, 390px, 430px, and desktop for overlap, overflow, density, long values, unavailable data, and Home Team states.
- Verify Value, Care, Team, More, Documents, Timeline, and Ask SuCasa links; keyboard focus; reduced motion; contrast; console output; type checks; and the full test suite.
