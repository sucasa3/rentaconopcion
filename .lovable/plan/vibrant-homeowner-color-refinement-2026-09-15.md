# Vibrant homeowner color refinement

## Goal
Make the approved light homeowner dashboard feel richer and more energetic without changing its structure, data, or navigation.

## Changes
- Strengthen homeowner-scoped navy, orange, intelligence blue, positive green, attention amber, and risk red tokens.
- Replace washed-out section accents with clearer, more saturated borders, icons, selected states, and compact tinted areas.
- Keep the page and primary cards light; preserve the property photo, frosted intelligence strip, section order, and current density.
- Keep unavailable data neutral. Green, amber, and red remain reserved for supported canonical conditions.
- Verify the dashboard at 320, 375, 390, 430, and desktop widths for contrast, overflow, and legibility.

## Not changing
- No new sections, metrics, statuses, gradients, or sample data.
- No calculation, relationship, permission, consent, or navigation changes.
- No changes to Agent or Lender views.
